QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Are you available 24/7?

Yes. R&J Multiservices provides roadside assistance 24/7. Call 346-390-4511, message us on WhatsApp, or send a request online.

What roadside services do you offer?

Jump starts, battery and alternator testing, battery installation, tire changes and plugs, lockout services, and fuel delivery.

What areas do you serve?

Houston, Katy, Richmond, Rosenberg, Cypress, Sugar Land, Stafford, Missouri City, and Fulshear, Texas.

How do I request help?

Call 346-390-4511, message us on WhatsApp, or complete the request form on this page.

What should I include in my request?

Your name and phone number, where the vehicle is located, what happened, and when you need help. You can also add photos of the situation.

I used R&J Multiservices. How can I share feedback?

Email services@autorj.com. With your permission, we may share your feedback on this site.
